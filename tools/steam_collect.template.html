Steam price history: manual collection

Nothing on this page contacts Steam. You open each link yourself, in a browser where you are logged in to Steam, and paste what it shows.

  1. Click Open price history for an item. A page of JSON opens in a new tab (if you see [] or an error, you are not logged in, or Steam is throttling: wait a minute).
  2. Copy all of it. In Firefox, choose Raw Data, then Copy. In Chrome, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
  3. Paste into that item's box. A green tick means it parsed, and the box empties itself.
  4. When you are done (or want to stop), click Download data and move the file to data/manual_steam/ in the project folder.

Progress is kept in this browser while you work. Items are interleaved across segments, so stopping part-way still gives a balanced sample. Go at a relaxed pace.
